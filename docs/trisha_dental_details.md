## 3. Services & Treatments Offered

1. **Root Canal Treatment (RCT)**
   - Painless therapy for infected pulp/decayed tooth preservation.
2. **Dental Crowns**
   - High-durability porcelain, ceramic, or zirconia crowns for restoring damaged/post-RCT teeth.
3. **Dental Implants**
   - Permanent tooth replacements using biocompatible titanium fixtures and natural-looking prosthetic teeth.
4. **Tooth Filling (Restorations)**
   - Composite and tooth-colored cosmetic fillings for cavities, chipping, and wear.
5. **Teeth Cleaning (Scaling & Polishing)**
   - Plaque and tartar removal, gum infection prevention, and ultrasonic scaling.
6. **Dental Braces & Aligners**
   - Orthodontic treatments including traditional metal/ceramic braces and clear invisible aligners for teeth straightening and malocclusion correction.
7. **Teeth Whitening**
   - Professional bleaching and stain removal to brighten and rejuvenate discolored teeth.
8. **Smile Designing (Veneers)**
   - Aesthetic smile makeovers using porcelain/composite veneers, contouring, and cosmetic alignment.
9. **Kids Dentistry (Pediatric Dental Care)**
   - Gentle, child-friendly preventive care, habit-breaking appliances, fluoride treatments, and cavity management.
